Common questions

Do you work with businesses outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes. Archon Tech is a remote-first team, so we work with clients anywhere, over calls and messages.

Can I get just one service, like social media only?

Yes. Each service works on its own, though most clients combine a website or app with ongoing social media support.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A simple site can take a couple of weeks; a custom web application takes longer. We'll give you a clear timeline after the discussion stage.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes, we stay available for fixes and updates once your project is live.

Not sure which service fits?

Tell us what you're trying to do, and we'll suggest the right starting point.
