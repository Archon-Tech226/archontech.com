A small, remote team that builds things properly.

Archon Tech started as a way to give small and growing businesses the kind of website and web application usually reserved for companies with much bigger budgets.

We work as a small, remote team, which keeps us close to every project instead of spreading thin across too many clients at once. Each site or application is built around what the business actually needs, not a template stretched to fit.

Alongside development, we manage social media for clients who want their online presence to feel as considered as their website does.

  • Built around the business

    We start by understanding how you work, then design and build around that, not the other way round.

  • Clear communication

    You see progress as we build, not just a finished product at the end.

  • Support after launch

    A project isn't done the day it goes live. We stay available for fixes and updates.
