How a project runs

A simple four-step process, with you involved at every stage.

  1. Discuss

    We learn what your business needs and agree on what to build.

  2. Design

    You see the layout and flow before any code is written.

  3. Build

    We develop it, share progress and test on real devices.

  4. Launch and support

    We put it live and stay available for fixes and updates.
